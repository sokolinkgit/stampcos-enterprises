About StampCos Enterprises

We Help Kenyan Businesses
Look Official — Instantly.

StampCos Enterprises is a Nairobi workshop at Queen Mall G17, Moi Avenue — opposite Bihi Towers. We design, engrave and assemble rubber stamps, company seals, date stamps, name tags, door tags and garment marking inks right in town — no brokers, no delays.

We started with one engraving machine and a promise: crisp impressions, honest prices, fast turnaround. Today schools, law firms, clinics, SACCOs, logistics companies and government offices trust us for stamps that are legible, durable, and legally compliant. What you approve on screen is exactly what you receive.

No fake story. Just real work: we proof every artwork, test every stamp on paper, and only release it when the impression is razor-sharp. If it’s not crisp, we re-make it — free.
