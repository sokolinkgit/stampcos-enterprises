About Us

Straight talk: this is what we actually do.

StampCos Enterprises is a Kenyan print-and-supply shop. We make and sell graphic T-shirts — anime, Mickey & cartoon, 3D effect prints, vintage styles and full custom prints from your own design — plus curated thrift clothing. We print on quality cotton, check every piece by hand, and deliver to every county in Kenya through riders and courier.

We are not going to feed you a fake "it all started in a garage in 2014" story. Here is the honest version: we saw that good graphic tees were either overpriced or low quality, and that getting a custom design printed meant endless back-and-forth. So we built a shop where you can see real prices, order in one WhatsApp message, pay via M-PESA, and actually receive what you paid for. That is the whole business.
